# Switchback vs AllTrails: North Bend, WA

Both apps searched the same area: the map view Switchback opens for "North Bend, Washington" (47.398–47.593 N, 121.720–121.854 W), on 2026-10-10.

- **AllTrails:** its connector returns at most 20 trails per search. The lists sorted by popularity and by distance held the same 20 trails.
- **Switchback:** found 71 named trails from OpenStreetMap.

**Tally:**

- **9** are AllTrails "outings" (a destination, loop or section). Switchback has the underlying trail but not that route.
- **3** are missing from Switchback, because those paths have no trail name in OpenStreetMap.

## What Switchback shows that AllTrails doesn't

- About 30 mountain-bike lines in Raging River and on Snoqualmie Ridge, such as Derby, FlowTron, Goose Chase and Tetherball. My bike-trail filter missed them because they aren't tagged as bike-only.
- Short connectors and campground paths under half a mile, such as Tanner, Bear, Wren and Sunrise.
- Utility tracks, such as Emergency Access Road and Gun Range.

## Main differences

1. **Curated routes:** AllTrails lists the hike people actually do, like "Mount Si Loop" or "to East Peak". Switchback lists the trails as they are mapped, so long trails show their whole length and loops built from several named trails appear as pieces.
2. **Climb:** where both have a number, Switchback's elevation gain is 10–25% lower than AllTrails', because its elevation data is coarse.
3. **Popularity and noise:** AllTrails ranks by popularity and drops bike lines and connectors. Switchback has no ratings and shows everything named, so its list is noisier.
